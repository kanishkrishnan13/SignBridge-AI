Step 1 — Environment Setup
  • Sit 50–80 cm from your camera
  • Ensure bright, even lighting — avoid backlighting
  • Use a plain, contrasting background where possible
  • Hold your right hand open, palm facing the camera
  • Make sure your entire hand is visible in the frame
💡 Natural daylight or a desk lamp pointing at your hand works best.
🖐️ Spread all five fingers slightly apart during calibration.
📏 Keep your elbow resting comfortably — no need to hold your arm up high.
🔄 Re-calibrate whenever you change location or lighting conditions.
Step 2 — Hold Still
Position your open hand in the centre of the frame and keep it still while we sample 30 frames.
